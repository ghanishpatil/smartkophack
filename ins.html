Welcome, Evaluator

Thank you for lending your expertise to Smart Kopargaon Hackathon.

Smart Kopargaon Hackathon (SKH) is an innovation movement that brings together students, government, MSMEs, mentors, and industry experts to co-create solutions for real civic and industrial challenges. As a member of the jury, you assess the teams assigned to you, score them against a structured rubric, record your remarks, and set each team’s qualification outcome.

This handbook covers everything you need: signing in, understanding your dashboard, reviewing team submissions, scoring fairly, and submitting your final evaluation. No technical background is required — the platform guides you at each step.

Round 2 Evaluation Criteria

Every submission is scored out of 100 marks across the seven criteria below. Assess each criterion independently on its own merits, using the descriptions as your guide. On the review screen each criterion has its own slider and maximum.

# Criterion Marks What to look for
1Problem Understanding & Relevance15Clarity of the problem, target users and relevance to the given problem statement.
2Innovation & Uniqueness15Originality, novelty and differentiation of the proposed solution.
3Technical Feasibility & Prototype20Technical soundness, appropriate technology and functionality / progress of the prototype.
4Impact & Effectiveness15Potential to create measurable social, economic, environmental or industrial impact.
5Scalability & Sustainability15Potential for wider adoption, scalability and long-term sustainability.
6User-Centricity & Usability10Ease of use, accessibility and alignment with the needs of intended users.
7Completeness & Quality of Submission10Quality, clarity and completeness of documentation, supporting evidence and overall presentation.
TOTAL 100 Sum of all seven criteria
Highest weight — Technical Feasibility & Prototype (20 marks) Give the working prototype and its demonstrated progress proportionate attention — it carries the most marks of any single criterion.
How the marks are applied On the review screen you simply move each criterion’s slider to the marks it deserves; the platform totals and normalizes the scores, so results stay fair across different problem statements.

Your Role & How Evaluation Works

Every jury member gets a scoped workspace. You only ever see the problem statements and teams that organizers assigned to you — there is no global directory, and nothing outside your scope is visible. This keeps evaluation focused and confidential.

Your responsibilities are simple:

  • Review each assigned team’s problem statement and submitted materials.
  • Score the team against the official rubric using the on-screen sliders.
  • Write remarks for the organizers and finalists.
  • Set the team’s outcome — Qualified Waitlist Not Qualified
  • Submit your final evaluation.
Fair scoring across rubrics Scores are automatically normalized (your total ÷ the rubric’s maximum), so results stay fair even if different problem statements use slightly different rubrics. You just move the sliders — the platform handles the maths.

Getting Started — Signing In

  1. Open your invitation emailOrganizers send your login credentials (email + a temporary password) to your registered address. Keep this email handy.
  2. Go to the SKH platformOpen the platform link from your invitation in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari), on desktop or mobile.
  3. Log in with your credentialsEnter your email and the temporary password provided.
  4. Set your own passwordOn first login you’ll be asked to create a new password. Choose something secure and private to you.

Trouble logging in? If the temporary password doesn’t work or the invitation hasn’t arrived, contact the organizing team — they can re-send credentials to your registered email.

Your Assignments & Scope

Open Assignments to see the problem statements you’re responsible for. Each card shows the problem’s organization, department, category (track) and theme (domain), followed by the teams that chose that problem. Use the problem filter at the top to focus on one statement at a time.

Every team row shows its current evaluation status and a Review button that opens the full evaluation screen.

No assignments showing? If you don’t see any problem statements or teams, organizers may not have attached them to your profile yet. Reach out to the organizing team and they’ll assign you.

The Evaluation Process, Step by Step

  1. Open the Evaluate Teams queueFrom the left menu select Evaluate Teams to see every team assigned to you.
  2. Pick a team and open its reviewClick Open review on any team to load its full evaluation screen.
  3. Study the problem & submissionRead the problem statement and review the team’s deck, PDF, demo video and repository links.
  4. Score against the rubricMove each slider to the score you feel the team earned on that criterion.
  5. Write your remarksAdd comments for organizers and finalists in the remarks box.
  6. Set the team statusChoose Qualified, Waitlist or Not Qualified. This is required.
  7. Submit your final evaluationClick Submit final. Your sheet is then locked for that team.

Inside the Team Review Screen

The review screen is split into two parts:

Left — The team’s work

  • Problem statement — full details and description.
  • Submission — buttons and previews for the Deck, PDF, Demo/Video and Repository.
  • Submission timeline — status, whether the team locked it, and last-updated time.

Right — Your evaluation

  • Rubric sliders — one per criterion, each with its score range.
  • Remarks — free-text notes for organizers / finalists.
  • Save draft and Submit final buttons.
Your work is saved as you go Drafts autosave while you score, and you’ll see an “Autosaved” time stamp. You can safely leave and return — nothing is lost until you submit.

Scoring, Remarks & Team Status

Rubric scoring

Each criterion has its own slider and maximum. Drag the slider to set your score — the current value shows beside the criterion name (for example 7.5 / 10). Read the short hint under each criterion to calibrate your judgement. The full Round 2 rubric — seven criteria totalling 100 marks — is on the Round 2 Evaluation Criteria page near the front of this handbook.

Remarks

Use the Remarks box to justify your scores and give constructive feedback. These notes help organizers and are valuable to finalists.

Team status — required

Before you can submit, you must set the team’s outcome:

StatusMeaning
QualifiedThe team advances / meets the bar to proceed.
WaitlistBorderline — held for consideration depending on capacity.
Not QualifiedThe team does not advance at this stage.
Status is mandatory The Submit final button stays disabled until you pick a status. This ensures every evaluated team receives a clear outcome.
8

Drafts, Final Submit & Locking

  • Save draft keeps your scores and remarks without finalizing — you can come back and edit anytime.
  • Submit final records your evaluation and locks your sheet for that team.
  • After submitting you cannot edit unless an organizer reopens your sheet.
Before you finalize Double-check your slider values, remarks and chosen status. Submit only when you’re confident — it’s your final word on that team.

Tracking Your Progress

Open Progress to see a completion percentage and a breakdown of your teams by status (Pending, In progress, Submitted, Locked). Both submitted and locked evaluations count as complete. The Home page mirrors these counts so you always know how many teams remain.

Frequently Asked Questions

I can’t edit a team’s scores — why?

Either you’ve already submitted (your sheet is locked), or the evaluation window is closed. Ask organizers to reopen if you need to make a change.

The Submit button is greyed out.

You haven’t set the team status yet. Choose Qualified, Waitlist or Not Qualified at the top of the review screen and the button will enable.

A submission link or preview won’t open.

Some teams share Google Drive or external links. Use the labelled buttons (Deck, PDF, Video, Repository) to open them in a new tab. If a preview fails to embed, use the direct link button instead.

I opened a team link and got “Access denied”.

Only open teams from your own dashboard. Links to teams outside your assigned scope are intentionally blocked.

Can I change a team’s status after submitting?

Not once your sheet is locked. Contact an organizer if a correction is genuinely needed — they can reopen it.

Do I need to evaluate teams in any particular order?

No. Work through your queue in whatever order suits you; the platform tracks each team independently.

Need help? For anything not covered here — credentials, assignments, reopening a sheet or technical issues — contact the SKH organizing team. They’re on hand throughout the evaluation window.
